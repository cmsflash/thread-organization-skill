# Maintaining this skill

Rules for anyone editing SKILL.md, README.md or `references/`.

- **Stateless.** The files describe the rules as they are now. No change
  log: no "since <date>", "replacing <old rule>", "used to" or "now".
- **No citations.** Rules are rules. Do not attribute a rule to a person or a
  date, quote the human's words as its source, cite the incident, thread or
  PR behind it, or say where a pattern was collected. When a reason helps,
  state it generically.
- **Trigger phrases are not citations.** The phrases the human types to ask
  for something ("use MPIR", "keep monitoring") stay, in the description and
  where a section says what it answers.
- **No names of people.** The person who approves is `HUMAN`.
- **Models that are free or self-hosted** are called that, never named by
  route.
- **No costs or billing figures** from real runs.
- **Paths never carry a username.** Write `~/…`.
- **Example titles are made up or generic.** `Train` and
  `Train: Main Migration` are fine.
- **Examples are synthetic.** They live under `references/examples/`, and
  never cite or copy real workspace files.
